Drop files here for the daily run:

- `manual.csv`: posts found by hand, ChatGPT or Muse (see `prompts/discovery.md`).
  Columns: url, views, likes, shares, saves, comments, followers, author, text, found_by.
- `asc-campaigns.csv`: App Store Connect > App Analytics > Acquisition > Campaigns,
  exported per day. Columns: date, campaign (the `ct`), page views, installs, proceeds.
