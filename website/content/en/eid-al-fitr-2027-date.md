Eid al-Fitr is one of the most anticipated days of the year. After a month of fasting, people look forward to prayer, family, and the first meal of the new month. Because Eid depends on the moon, many people ask early which day it will fall on.

This guide explains the expected date for 2027, why the day can change, and how to confirm it for your country.

## The expected date

Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin on Monday, 8 February 2027. If Ramadan has 29 days, the last day of the month falls on 8 March, and Eid al-Fitr is expected on Tuesday, 9 March 2027. If Ramadan has 30 days, Eid is expected on Wednesday, 10 March 2027.

These are expected dates, not confirmed ones. Some countries and communities may publish a different day, so do not finalise plans until you have checked.

## Why the date can change

Islamic months begin when the new crescent moon is sighted, or when the month of Sha'ban completes thirty days. This means the start of Shawwal, and therefore Eid, depends on the sighting in each place. Some countries use local sighting, and others use calculation. The result is that neighbouring communities can celebrate on different days.

For this reason, it is wise to plan with the expected date in mind, and to update your plans when the announcement arrives.

## How to confirm the date

Follow these steps once Ramadan has begun:

1. Check the official announcement from your country's moon-sighting committee or religious authority.
2. Look for a clear statement that gives the day of Eid, not only the start of Ramadan.
3. Confirm with your local mosque, which usually shares the same date.
4. Keep a note of the date in your calendar, and set a reminder for the evening before.

## Plan the practical side

Eid planning is simple if you start early. Arrange your travel, your family visits and your work leave with a little flexibility. If you are travelling, book transport that you can change, and keep your plans open until the official date is clear.

It also helps to plan the Eid prayer. Find the time and place of the Eid prayer in your area, and plan to arrive early. Many mosques hold the prayer in the morning, shortly after sunrise.

## Plan the last days of Ramadan

The days before Eid are part of Ramadan, so keep your worship steady. Read our guide on [the Ramadan 2027 calendar](/blog/en/ramadan-2027-calendar/) to see how the last ten nights fall, and on [Laylatul Qadr 2027](/blog/en/laylatul-qadr-2027/) for the odd nights.

## A common mistake

Some people wait until the last day to plan Eid, then find that the Eid prayer, the travel, or the shopping is rushed. A better plan is to set three dates: the first expected date, the official announcement date, and the Eid prayer time. Then plan around those.

## Eid morning, step by step

A calm Eid morning starts the night before. Set your alarm for a time that gives you enough room to bathe, dress, and eat something light before you leave. Many people also make a small plan for the sadaqah they want to give, so the charity happens with the joy of the day rather than as a rushed decision.

On the morning itself, say the takbeers on your way to the prayer, pray Eid with the congregation, and then listen to the khutbah. After the prayer, greet your family and neighbours, and visit those who are alone or unwell. These simple acts make the day feel like the celebration it is meant to be.
