Athan by IslamicFinder is one of the most popular prayer apps, rated 4.8 on the US App Store from over 40,000 ratings, and its library of adhan voices is genuinely good. People look for an Athan app alternative for three reasons: the ads, the data it collects, and an adhan that is easy to sleep through at Fajr.

Here is where Athan falls short and which alternative fits what you need. App Store details were checked on 26 October 2026.

## Where the Athan app falls short

### Ads, unless you pay

The free version shows ads. On the US App Store you can buy "Remove Ads" for $3.99, or Athan Premium for $1.99 a month or $9.99 a year. The prices are fair, but ads around your prayer times are the most common complaint in Athan's reviews.

### What it collects

Athan's App Store privacy label lists **contact info** under "Data Used to Track You", and purchases, contact info, user content and identifiers as data linked to you. None of that is unusual for a free, ad-supported app, but it is more than a prayer-times app needs.

### It is a notification, not an alarm

This is the real problem for Fajr. Athan plays the adhan as an alert. It tells you it is time, and one tap silences it. At 5 a.m. your half-asleep brain will tap it without you ever really waking up. The listing does not describe any way to make the Fajr adhan harder to dismiss.

### It is a big download

Athan is listed at 298 MB, a lot for an app most people open to check five times a day.

### Salah Alarm: for Fajr

We make Salah Alarm, so weigh this accordingly. It gives you exact prayer times for your city, the adhan, Qibla, Quran and dua widgets with no ads, and it is built around waking you for Fajr. The adhan plays as an alarm at the exact Fajr time and only stops when you scan your prayer mat with your camera. In strict mode there is no snooze. SalahLock can block distracting apps at prayer time until you have prayed.

### Pillars: for clean, private prayer times

Pillars is ad-free, says it avoids collecting data, and is well designed, with widgets and a prayer tracker. It is a great Athan replacement if you just want accurate times. It is not an alarm.

### Muslim Pro: if you want everything in one app

Muslim Pro does more than Athan, with Quran, community and content features, but it has the same ads and a heavier tracking label (location and identifiers). Read our [Muslim Pro alternative](/blog/en/muslim-pro-alternative/) guide before you switch to it.
