Laylatul Qadr, the Night of Decree, is the night the Qur'an began to be revealed, and the Qur'an says that it is better than a thousand months. Many Muslims want to be sure they do not miss it. The good news is that the Prophet ﷺ told us where to look, and a steady plan for the last ten nights makes that search practical.

This guide gives the expected dates for 2027, the guidance on when to look, and a simple plan for the nights.

## Expected dates for 2027

Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin on Monday, 8 February 2027, with the first night of taraweeh on Sunday, 7 February. If that holds, the last ten nights begin on the evening of 27 February. The odd nights in that stretch are:

- The evening of Saturday, 27 February (night 21)
- The evening of Monday, 1 March (night 23)
- The evening of Wednesday, 3 March (night 25)
- The evening of Friday, 5 March (night 27)
- The evening of Sunday, 7 March (night 29)

These are expected dates. Your own country's moon sighting decides the actual start of Ramadan, and that moves the nights by a day. Check the official announcement for your country.

## Where the Prophet ﷺ told us to look

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Seek Laylatul Qadr in the odd nights of the last ten nights of Ramadan" (Sahih al-Bukhari 2017). The guidance is to search across the whole run of odd nights, not to fix on one. That is why a plan for all five is the most reliable approach.

## A plan for the last ten nights

Think of the last ten nights as a set of evenings, each one an opportunity. A simple plan helps you keep going through them.

1. **Set a sleep plan for the week.** Long night prayers are tiring, so protect your sleep in the days before.
2. **Make your nightly goal small and steady.** Two or four rak'ahs of night prayer, a few pages of Qur'an, and a set of dua is better than a long night you cannot repeat.
3. **Use the odd nights as a focus.** On those evenings, add extra time: longer dua, a longer recitation or a short act of charity.
4. **Keep the even nights too.** The guidance is to seek the night in the odd nights, but the other nights are still nights of worship.

## Dua for the night

The Prophet ﷺ taught his wife Aisha radhiyallahu 'anha a dua to say if she found Laylatul Qadr: "O Allah, You are Pardoning and love pardon, so pardon me" (Sunan al-Tirmidhi 3513). It is short, easy to memorise and worth repeating on every odd night.

## Plan the practical side

Good preparation makes the nights easier. Plan your work and family commitments for the final week, if you can. Set alarms for suhoor and Fajr, and keep the phone across the room. An alarm that calculates the real Fajr time, such as the one in Salah Alarm, keeps the morning simple after a late night of worship.

## Summary

- The last ten nights of Ramadan 2027 are expected to begin on the evening of 27 February.
- The odd nights to seek it in are expected to be 27 February, 1, 3, 5 and 7 March.
- Keep your nightly worship steady, and add more on the odd nights.
- Say the dua the Prophet ﷺ taught for this night.
