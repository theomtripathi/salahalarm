Finding the qibla, the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah, is one of the first things people want from a prayer app. An iPhone can help with this, because its compass and sensors can show you the direction in a few seconds. But a phone is only as accurate as its surroundings, and a few small steps make a real difference.

This guide explains how a qibla app works, how to calibrate it, and what to check before you rely on it.

## How a qibla compass works

A qibla compass combines your location with the phone's compass to show the direction of Makkah from where you stand. Your location tells the app where the Kaaba is relative to you. The compass tells the app which way your phone is facing. The app then points you toward the qibla.

Because it depends on both, the result can only be as good as the two inputs. A wrong location or a disturbed compass will give a wrong direction.

## Calibrate the compass first

Before you use the qibla compass, calibrate it. Most phones show a prompt to move the phone in a figure-eight motion. Do this in a clear space, away from metal.

If the compass seems off, try again. A few seconds of calibration usually fixes small errors.

## What affects accuracy

- **Metal objects nearby,** such as a desk, a car or a metal table, can pull the compass off.
- **Magnets and speakers** can disturb the sensor.
- **Your location,** if the app has not got it right, will shift the direction.
- **Standing near tall buildings or steel structures** can also reduce accuracy.

Step away from these, and check the direction again.

## Check against a mosque

If you are not sure, the simplest check is a mosque. The direction of the qibla is fixed for each place, so a mosque in your area will have the direction marked in its prayer hall. If your app disagrees, trust the mosque, and check your location setting.

## Using it in a new city

The qibla changes as you move. If you travel to another city or country, check the direction again, because the app will need your new location. Many people forget this when they arrive at a hotel, and find that the old direction is no longer correct.

## Why the qibla matters for every prayer

Many people only check the qibla once, when they move into a home, and never again. But the direction is part of every prayer you pray, so it is worth checking it properly once and then keeping a habit of rechecking when your surroundings change. A new desk, a new room or a new building can all change how the compass behaves.

If you pray at work, check the direction from your usual spot once, and mark it in a way you can find again, such as a small sticker on the wall or a note in your phone. That saves you a new calibration each day.

## A simple checklist

1. Allow the app to use your location.
2. Calibrate the compass with a figure-eight motion.
3. Move away from metal and magnets.
4. Check the direction against a mosque in your area.
5. Recheck when you travel.
