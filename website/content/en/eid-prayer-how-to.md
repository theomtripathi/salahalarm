The Eid prayer is two rakahs prayed in congregation on the morning of Eid. There is no adhan or iqamah. The imam says extra takbirs at the start of each rakah, recites aloud, and gives a khutbah after the prayer. You follow the imam, raising your hands with each takbir if your school does so.

Eid al-Fitr 2027 is expected on Tuesday 9 March or Wednesday 10 March, depending on the moon sighting. This guide walks through the prayer step by step, explains the extra takbirs, and covers the sunnahs of the morning so you are ready on the day.

## When the Eid prayer starts

The time for the Eid prayer begins once the sun has fully risen, roughly 15 to 20 minutes after sunrise, and lasts until just before Dhuhr. Most mosques and Eid grounds hold one or more jamaat between these times and publish them a few days before Eid.

Arrive early. Eid crowds are large, parking fills quickly, and it is sunnah to say the takbirs of Eid while you wait.

## Step by step: how to pray the Eid prayer

The steps below follow the most common practice. Scholars differ on the number of extra takbirs, so follow the imam of the jamaat you pray with.

1. **Make the intention** in your heart to pray the two rakahs of Eid behind the imam.
2. **Opening takbir.** Raise your hands and say Allahu akbar with the imam, as in any prayer.
3. **Extra takbirs in the first rakah.** The imam says several more takbirs before reciting. Many follow seven in total in the first rakah. Say each one quietly with the imam. Between them you may praise Allah quietly.
4. **Recitation.** The imam recites al-Fatihah and another surah aloud. You listen.
5. **Complete the first rakah** with ruku and two sujood as usual.
6. **Extra takbirs in the second rakah.** On standing, the imam says more takbirs, commonly five, before reciting.
7. **Recitation, ruku and sujood,** then the final sitting and taslim.
8. **Stay for the khutbah.** The khutbah comes after the prayer. Listening to it is part of the Eid sunnah.

### The Hanafi way

In the Hanafi school there are three extra takbirs in each rakah. In the first rakah they come after the opening takbir and before the recitation. In the second rakah they come after the recitation and before going into ruku. If you pray at a Hanafi mosque, the imam will say them in this order.

## Sunnahs of the morning of Eid

Eid al-Fitr has a set of simple sunnahs that make the morning special:

- **Ghusl** and wearing your best clean clothes.
- **Eat before you go.** The Prophet ﷺ did not go out on the day of Eid al-Fitr until he had eaten some dates, an odd number of them (Sahih al-Bukhari 953).
- **Pay zakat al-fitr before the prayer,** so it reaches people in need in time for Eid.
- **Say the takbirs of Eid** on the way and while you wait: Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar, la ilaha illallah, wallahu akbar, Allahu akbar, wa lillahil hamd.
- **Take a different route back.** The Prophet ﷺ used to return from the Eid prayer by a different way from the one he went by (Sahih al-Bukhari 986).
- **Bring the family.** Children, women and elders are all encouraged to attend.

## A simple Eid morning plan

1. **Pray Fajr on time.** It is easy to sleep in after the last nights of Ramadan, so set a reliable alarm.
2. **Ghusl, clothes and perfume** straight after Fajr.
3. **Eat a few dates** and pay zakat al-fitr if you have not already.
4. **Leave early** with time for parking and the takbirs.
5. **Pray, listen to the khutbah,** greet people, and return by another route.

## Common questions on the day

**Arrived late?** If you join while the imam is still in the prayer, join and follow him. If you missed the extra takbirs, many scholars say you do not need to make them up.

**Missed the prayer completely?** Scholars differ. Many allow praying two rakahs at home. Ask your local imam for the view your community follows.

**Praying at home?** If there is no Eid prayer near you, ask a local scholar how to mark the day. Many communities hold several jamaat so everyone can attend.

## Summary

- Eid prayer is two rakahs with extra takbirs, no adhan or iqamah, and a khutbah after.
- Many follow seven takbirs in the first rakah and five in the second; Hanafis say three in each.
- Its time starts after sunrise and ends before Dhuhr.
- Eat dates, pay zakat al-fitr, say the takbirs and take a different route home.
