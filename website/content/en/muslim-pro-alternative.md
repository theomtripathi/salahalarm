Muslim Pro is the biggest Muslim app in the world, with over 600,000 ratings on the US App Store alone. But a lot of people go looking for a Muslim Pro alternative, and the reasons are usually the same: too many ads, worries about tracking, a heavy app that tries to do everything, and an adhan that does not actually get them up for Fajr.

If that sounds like you, here is an honest look at where Muslim Pro falls short, and what to use instead depending on what you need. All App Store details below were checked on 26 October 2026; prices vary by country.

## Where Muslim Pro falls short

### 1. Ads in the middle of your worship

The free version of Muslim Pro contains ads, and its App Store listing sells Premium as an "ad-free experience". Ads next to the Quran or your prayer times are the most common complaint in its reviews. You can remove them, but only by paying: Premium is listed at $12.99 a month or $34.99 a year in the US.

### 2. Tracking, and a trust problem that has not gone away

Muslim Pro's own App Store privacy label lists **location** and **identifiers** under "Data Used to Track You", meaning they can be linked with data from other companies' apps and websites.

That matters because of what happened in 2020. Vice reported that location data from Muslim Pro reached a data broker, X-Mode, whose data was sold on to contractors working for the US military. Muslim Pro denied selling personal data, said what it shared was anonymised, and ended its relationships with X-Mode and all other data partners. Still, for many Muslims it was a reminder to check what a prayer app collects before giving it their location five times a day.

### 3. It is huge

Muslim Pro is a Quran app, prayer app, community app, Islamic calendar, halal places guide and more. The App Store lists it at 372 MB. That is fine if you use all of it. If you open it twice a day to check prayer times, you are carrying a lot of app for very little.

### 4. The adhan tells you it is time, but it does not wake you up

This is the one that costs people Fajr. Muslim Pro's adhan is an alert: it plays, you tap it away, and if you are half asleep you will not even remember doing it. Its listing does not describe anything you have to do to stop the Fajr adhan. For Isha or Asr that is fine. For Fajr, an alert you can dismiss in your sleep is exactly the problem.

### 5. Premium is expensive for what most people use

Most people use a prayer app for three things: prayer times, the adhan and the Qibla. Paying up to $34.99 a year mainly to remove ads from those is a hard sell.

### Salah Alarm: if you want to stop missing Fajr

Salah Alarm is the alternative we built, so take this with that in mind. It does the everyday things Muslim Pro does, exact prayer times for your city with the major calculation methods, the adhan, Qibla, Quran and dua widgets, but it is built around one problem: getting you out of bed for Fajr.

At Fajr the adhan plays as an alarm, and it only stops when you scan your prayer mat with your phone camera. By the time it is quiet you are up, standing where you pray. In strict mode there is no snooze. SalahLock can also block the apps that pull you away from salah until you have prayed. There are no ads.

### Pillars: if you want a clean, private prayer-times app

Pillars is a well-made, ad-free app from a small independent team that says it avoids collecting data in the first place. It covers prayer times, Qibla, widgets and a prayer tracker. It is a good choice if all you want is accurate times without the noise. It is not built to wake you up, so for Fajr you will still need a proper alarm.

### Athan by IslamicFinder: if you mainly want adhan voices

Athan has a large library of adhan recitations and is rated 4.8 in the US. But it has the same trade-offs as Muslim Pro: the free version contains ads ("Remove Ads" is $3.99), its privacy label lists contact info as used to track you, and the adhan is an alert rather than a wake-up alarm. See our [Athan app alternative](/blog/en/athan-app-alternative/) guide for more.

## Which one should you pick?

1. **You keep missing Fajr:** Salah Alarm. It is the only one of these that will not let you switch the alarm off from bed.
2. **You want accurate times and nothing else:** Pillars.
3. **You use Muslim Pro's Quran, community and content features every day:** stay on Muslim Pro and consider Premium, but review its privacy settings.

Whichever you choose, the habit matters more than the app. Our guide on [how to wake up for Fajr](/blog/en/how-to-wake-up-for-fajr/) covers the sleep, Sunnah and routine side, which no app can do for you.
